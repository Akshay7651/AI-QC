* `data/` is being filled in the background by `fetch_dataset.py` (resumable, ~12,000 rows, may still be filling while you work; use what exists, re-scan periodically):
  - data/forms/<docket>.jpg  the signed form (photo/scan of the paper "Proforma-3", ~1597x1964 JPEG, handwritten Hindi/English, photographed under varied light/skew/rotation)
  - data/photos/<docket>_<i>.jpg  up to 3 evidence photos (720x1280) for ~1,500 rows

* Proforma-3 layout: printed barcode top-right with a FORM NO printed below it (pattern `HR0126` + 6 digits, e.g. HR0126175631); "PO ID:" handwritten = the 18-digit docket id (e.g. 040106260000636479) - a FREE LABEL for handwritten digits; numbered fields 1-15 (farmer name, crop, survey no, sowing date, loss date, intimation date, committee-inspection date, policy no); a table whose row 1 (sometimes row 2) holds survey no, insured area, AFFECTED AREA %, LOSS %; a total row "कुल:" under the table (may be blank); four signature blocks at the bottom: farmer (कृषक), loss-assessor company (लॉस एसेसर कम्पनी), primary worker (प्राथमिक कार्यकर्ता), block agriculture officer (खण्ड कृषि अधिकारी; sometimes only a printed rubber stamp, which is NOT a signature). Values are mostly "0"/"00", or 2-digit multiples like 20, 30, 70, 80, 100, sometimes decimals like 0.01 or "30%". The form is sometimes photographed with a different page position/rotation.
* The evidence photos carry a burned-in stamp at the bottom: "Latitude: 29.4562699 / Longitude: 75.7481476 / GPS Accuracy / Date: 21-09-2026 02:04 PM". Some uploaded "field photos" are actually photos of the paper form on a table/cloth (QC must flag these), some are duplicated, some are rotated 90 degrees, some are real field photos (standing crop / lodged / waterlogged / cut & spread / bare).
